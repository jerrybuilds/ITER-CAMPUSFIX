# CampusFix – SOA ITER | Campus Grievance Management System

CampusFix is a centralized campus complaint management platform designed for students and staff of SOA ITER. It provides a secure and organized way to report campus-related issues, track complaints, and monitor their resolution progress.

Users can register or log in using their unique registration ID and official "@soa.in" email address, with OTP-based email verification to ensure authorized access. The platform allows users to submit complaints by specifying their department, hostel, room number, complaint category, and other relevant details.

From academic and hostel issues to campus facilities and maintenance concerns, CampusFix helps users report problems efficiently while keeping track of complaint status, updates, and resolutions. It aims to improve communication between the campus community and the administration through a transparent, accountable, and user-friendly grievance reporting system.

Key Features:

- Secure login with official "@soa.in" email and OTP verification.
- Unique registration ID-based user identification.
- Complaint submission with department, hostel, room number, and issue details.
- Complaint status tracking from submission to resolution.
- Organized grievance management for students and staff.
- Improved transparency, accountability, and campus communication.

Goal: To build a smarter, more responsive campus where every genuine concern has a clear path toward resolution.
